01 — The Promise

Four vows, struck in iron.

  1. I

    Never stop building.

    Small or large, finished or rough. Something leaves the forge every season.

  2. II

    Heat, strike, repeat.

    Progress comes from repetition. Every attempt hardens the craft a little more.

  3. III

    Return to harbor.

    Rest is part of the work. The harbor is where I reflect, repair and set out again.

  4. IV

    Build in the open.

    Share the sparks and the slag. Honest logs beat polished silence.
